Frequently Asked Questions

How can I buy match tickets?

Match tickets can be purchased through our official ticketing partners. Visit the Matches section for upcoming fixtures and booking links.

Where can I buy official merchandise?

Visit our Merchandise section for the complete range of official Team India products with secure online payment and fast delivery.

How do I join the Fanzone?

Simply create an account by clicking the Login button and signing up. Enjoy exclusive content, polls, and connect with fellow fans.

Can I visit the BCCI office?

The BCCI office is not open for general public visits. For specific inquiries, please contact us via phone or email.

How do I report a technical issue?

For technical issues with the website, please email support@teamindia.com with details of the problem and screenshots if possible.

Media and Press Inquiries?

For media inquiries and press releases, please contact our media team at media@teamindia.com or call +91 22 2289 8802.
