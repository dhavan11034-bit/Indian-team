ACHIEVEMENTS

ICC Tournament Victories

Champions on the world stage

ODI World Cup 1983

Venue: Lord's, England

Captain: Kapil Dev

India stunned the cricketing world by defeating the mighty West Indies in the final. Mohinder Amarnath was the Man of the Match in the final.

India 183 vs West Indies 140

T20 World Cup 2007

Venue: Johannesburg, South Africa

Captain: MS Dhoni

India won the inaugural T20 World Cup, defeating Pakistan in a thrilling final. Irfan Pathan and RP Singh were the bowling heroes.

India 157/5 vs Pakistan 152

ODI World Cup 2011

Venue: Wankhede Stadium, Mumbai

Captain: MS Dhoni

India lifted the World Cup after 28 years on home soil. MS Dhoni's iconic six sealed the victory. Gautam Gambhir scored a crucial 97.

India 277/4 vs Sri Lanka 274/6

Champions Trophy 2013

Venue: Edgbaston, Birmingham

Captain: MS Dhoni

India dominated throughout the tournament and defeated England in the final. Ravindra Jadeja and Ishant Sharma were the bowling stars.

India 129/7 vs England 124

T20 World Cup 2024

Venue: Barbados, West Indies

Captain: Rohit Sharma

India ended an 11-year ICC trophy drought with a thrilling victory over South Africa. Hardik Pandya and Jasprit Bumrah were exceptional in the final overs.

Champions 2024

ODI World Cup 2003

Venue: South Africa

Status: Runners-Up

India had a dream run with Sachin Tendulkar in sublime form. Reached the final before losing to Australia. Memorable victories against Pakistan and England.

Historic Test Series Victories

🇦🇺 Australia 2018-19

First-ever Test series win in Australia (2-1). Cheteshwar Pujara and Jasprit Bumrah were outstanding.

🇦🇺 Australia 2020-21

Historic series win at the Gabba (2-1) despite injuries. Rishabh Pant's heroic innings sealed the victory.

🇬🇧 England 2007

Test series win in England after 21 years. Zaheer Khan's bowling was exceptional throughout the series.

🇬🇧 England 2021

Dominant 2-1 series lead before the final Test. Brilliant performances across batting and bowling.

🇿🇦 South Africa 2006-07

First Test series win in South Africa. Sachin Tendulkar and VVS Laxman scored crucial centuries.

🇿🇦 South Africa 2018-19

First-ever Test series win in South Africa (2-1). Virat Kohli led from the front with aggressive batting.

Iconic Test Matches

Eden Gardens 2001

vs Australia

Following on, VVS Laxman (281) and Rahul Dravid (180) scripted the greatest Test comeback. India won by 171 runs after being forced to follow on.

Adelaide 2003

vs Australia

Rahul Dravid (233) and VVS Laxman (148) batted throughout the final day to save the Test match. One of the greatest rearguard actions in Test history.

Chennai 1999

vs Pakistan

Anil Kumble took all 10 wickets in an innings, only the second bowler in Test history to achieve this feat.
